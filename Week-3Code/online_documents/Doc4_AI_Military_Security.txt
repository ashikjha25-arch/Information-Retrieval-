Title: AI Military Risks Spark Concern at Security Conferences Worldwide
Source: Reuters / BBC
Date: 2026-09-17

Delegates at major defense conferences expressed fears over the potential for unchecked AI development to heighten global security risks. US and China security experts have proposed nuclear-style safeguards for AI risks, drawing parallels between the dangers of advanced artificial intelligence and nuclear weapons. The discussions at China's flagship defense conference revealed deep concerns about technology rivalries and the lack of international frameworks governing autonomous weapons systems. AI-powered drones are transforming modern warfare, and delegates warned that without proper safeguards, the technology could lead to unintended escalations. The British Broadcasting Corporation reported that AI military applications are a top priority at security summits worldwide. The call for international regulation mirrors the nuclear non-proliferation treaty model, with experts arguing that AI weapons require similar oversight to prevent catastrophic consequences.
