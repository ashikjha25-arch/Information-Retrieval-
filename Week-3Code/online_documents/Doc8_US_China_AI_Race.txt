Title: US-China AI Competition Intensifies as Huawei Launches New AI Chips
Source: Reuters / Guardian / New York Times
Date: 2026-09-17

The artificial intelligence race between the United States and China is intensifying as Huawei Technologies announces it cannot produce enough AI computing equipment to meet demand in China. The Chinese tech giant is stepping up its challenge to Nvidia, launching two new AI chips for 2027. Huawei's rotating chairman Eric Xu stated that Chinese AI is not yet powerful enough to see frontier risks, while US security experts propose nuclear-style safeguards for AI risks. The New York Times reported that the debate over AI regulation is dividing leaders in both Silicon Valley and Washington. The Guardian noted that the AI competition has hardened into increasingly entrenched rival camps, with some experts warning that AI could kill all humans in the next decade. China's Huawei claims AI chip demand outstrips supply and is limiting overseas sales. The rivalry extends beyond hardware to software, with Microsoft AI chief Mustafa Suleyman calling out Anthropic's approach to AI consciousness as potentially dangerous. The competition raises fundamental questions about global AI governance and whether any country can develop safe AI in isolation.
