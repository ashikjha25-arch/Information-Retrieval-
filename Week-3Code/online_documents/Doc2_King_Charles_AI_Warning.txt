Title: King Charles Warns AI Leaders of Existential Dangers
Source: Reuters / BBC
Date: 2026-09-17

Britain's King Charles warned AI leaders at a summit in Scotland of the existential dangers posed by artificial intelligence falling into the wrong hands. He said safeguards were needed before it was too late, addressing participants from major AI companies including Nvidia, OpenAI, and Anthropic. The King's remarks came amid growing concerns about the rapid development of AI technology without adequate oversight. He emphasized that the world cannot afford to be complacent about the risks of advanced AI systems and called for urgent international cooperation to ensure these powerful technologies are developed and deployed responsibly. The summit highlighted the growing recognition that AI safety is not just a technical challenge but a global priority requiring coordinated action from governments, industry, and civil society.
