Title: EU Proposes Under-13s Social Media Ban as Nations Crack Down on AI-Powered Platforms
Source: Reuters / BBC / New York Times
Date: 2026-09-17

European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen proposed a sweeping plan to keep children safe from online dangers, including a ban on under-13s from social media platforms. The European Union also outlined age limits for social media use across the 27-nation bloc. These measures come as AI-powered algorithms increasingly shape what children see online, and concerns grow about the impact of social media on young people's mental health. The BBC reported that UK teens are divided on the social media ban, with some seeing it as necessary protection and others as unnecessary restriction. The New York Times noted that social media companies are facing increasing pressure from regulators worldwide as evidence mounts about the harmful effects of algorithmic content amplification on minors. The proposals also include requirements for online services to design safe children's accounts, reflecting growing acknowledgment that current platforms are inadequate for protecting younger users from the sophisticated AI systems that curate their online experiences.
